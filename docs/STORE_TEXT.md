## itch.io description

Nobody reels in a fish without a fight. Fish are slippery, so the moment your hook touches one, you are in a duel, and your very serious samurai has to win it before the timer runs out.

Win, and the fish becomes nigiri. Sell your catch, sharpen the katana, let out more line and meet bigger, angrier fish. There are nine kinds, from a sardine to a giant squid, and three upgrade tracks with no end. A round takes under a minute. Your progress is saved in the browser.

Slippery fish dodge when they are ready to slip, so bait the dodge with a quick slash and then land a heavy one. Biters hit back, so watch for the wind-up and block or dash.

## itch.io controls line

Keyboard or gamepad, switch any time. Keyboard: A D or arrows to move, Space or Enter to confirm, J quick slash, K heavy slash, L or Shift to block (tap with a direction to dodge), E for the shop.
